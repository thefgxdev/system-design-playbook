# ADR-NNNN: Title in the form of a decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names
- **Reversibility:** cheap (hours) | moderate (a sprint) | expensive (a quarter or more)

## Context

What is the situation, what forces are in play, what constraint makes this decision necessary now. Two paragraphs at most. Link to the incident, the metric or the requirement.

## Options considered

1. **Option A.** One line on what it is. Pros. Cons. Cost.
2. **Option B.** Same.
3. **Do nothing.** Always listed. What happens if we decide later.

## Decision

The option chosen, in one sentence, followed by the two or three reasons that actually decided it.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder.
- What we must do because of this (tickets, follow-ups, the contract step of a migration).
- What would make us revisit this decision.
